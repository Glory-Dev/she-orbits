The Space Technology sector explores the engineering innovations that make modern space exploration possible. Participants study spacecraft systems, onboard technologies, avionics, sensors, propulsion support systems, mission operations, and emerging technological advancements. Emphasis is placed on understanding how engineering solutions are developed to overcome the challenges of operating in the harsh space environment.
