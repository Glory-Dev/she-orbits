The STEM sector serves as the educational foundation of SHE ORBITS, empowering learners through interdisciplinary education in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. Designed to cultivate analytical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills, this sector introduces participants to the core principles that drive innovation across the global space ecosystem. Through practical projects, technical workshops, and collaborative learning experiences, participants develop the confidence and knowledge required to pursue academic excellence, research, and careers in aerospace, engineering, and emerging technologies.
