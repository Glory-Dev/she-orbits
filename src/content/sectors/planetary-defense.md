The Planetary Defense & Asteroid Science sector focuses on the detection, characterization, and mitigation of near-Earth objects. Participants learn about asteroid dynamics, impact risk assessment, planetary protection strategies, space surveillance systems, and international efforts to safeguard Earth from potential asteroid threats.
