The Space Agriculture sector investigates sustainable food production systems for long-duration space missions and extraterrestrial habitats. Participants explore controlled-environment agriculture, hydroponics, aeroponics, plant biology, closed-loop ecosystems, and food security strategies essential for establishing permanent human settlements on the Moon, Mars, and beyond.
