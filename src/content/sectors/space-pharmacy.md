The Space Pharmacy sector explores pharmaceutical sciences within the context of space exploration. Participants learn how medications behave under microgravity conditions, how pharmaceutical manufacturing may evolve in space, and the challenges of maintaining astronaut health during extended missions. The sector highlights the growing importance of pharmaceutical innovation for future human exploration.
