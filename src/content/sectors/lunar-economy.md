The Lunar Economy & Resource Utilization sector explores the emerging opportunities surrounding sustainable lunar exploration and commercialization. Participants examine in-situ resource utilization (ISRU), lunar mining, infrastructure development, transportation systems, economic models, and the role of the Moon in enabling future deep-space exploration.
