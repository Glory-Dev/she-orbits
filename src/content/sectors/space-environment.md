The Space Environment sector examines the natural conditions that influence spacecraft performance and astronaut safety. Topics include space weather, solar activity, radiation, plasma environments, orbital debris, atmospheric drag, and environmental monitoring. Participants gain an understanding of how these factors affect mission planning and spacecraft operations.
