The Space Industry sector provides comprehensive insights into the rapidly evolving global space economy, exploring the organizations, technologies, and commercial opportunities shaping the future of humanity beyond Earth. Participants gain an understanding of government agencies, private space companies, NewSpace entrepreneurship, mission development, satellite services, launch operations, and international collaboration. This sector prepares future professionals to navigate and contribute to one of the world's fastest-growing industries.
