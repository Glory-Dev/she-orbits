The Philosophy of Space & Cultural Heritage sector encourages participants to examine humanity's relationship with the cosmos from philosophical, historical, ethical, and cultural perspectives. It explores the societal significance of space exploration, the preservation of humanity's heritage beyond Earth, and the role of space in shaping our collective future.
