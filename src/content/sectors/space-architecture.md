The Space Architecture & Habitat Design sector focuses on designing safe, sustainable, and functional living environments for astronauts beyond Earth. Participants study habitat engineering, life support integration, structural design, environmental control systems, human-centered architecture, and future concepts for lunar bases, Martian colonies, and deep-space stations.
