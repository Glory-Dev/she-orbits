The Space Navigation sector focuses on the science and engineering behind spacecraft motion, orbital mechanics, trajectory design, and navigation systems. Participants explore orbital transfers, mission planning, spacecraft guidance, GNSS technologies, and the mathematical principles governing spaceflight. Through computational tools and real-world mission scenarios, learners develop the skills necessary for designing and analyzing missions in Earth orbit and deep space.
