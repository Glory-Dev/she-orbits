The Space Medicine sector examines the physiological and medical challenges associated with human spaceflight. Participants investigate the effects of microgravity, radiation exposure, isolation, altered circadian rhythms, and long-duration missions on the human body. The sector also explores countermeasures, astronaut healthcare, biomedical monitoring, and the future of medical support for lunar and interplanetary missions.
