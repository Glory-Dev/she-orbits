The Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) sector explores satellite-based positioning, navigation, and timing technologies alongside tools to collect, analyze, visualize, and interpret spatial data. Participants learn about GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou, digital mapping, geospatial analysis, and real-world applications supporting transportation, Earth observation, robotics, and autonomous systems.
