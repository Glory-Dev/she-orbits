The Earth Observation & Climate Science sector demonstrates how satellite technologies contribute to understanding and protecting our planet. Participants explore remote sensing, climate monitoring, disaster management, environmental assessment, land-use analysis, and geospatial intelligence while learning how satellite data supports evidence-based decision making for global sustainability.
