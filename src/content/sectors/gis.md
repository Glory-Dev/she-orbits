The Geographic Information Systems (GIS) sector equips participants with the tools and techniques used to collect, analyze, visualize, and interpret spatial data. Learners develop practical skills in digital mapping, geospatial analysis, satellite imagery interpretation, and decision support for environmental and engineering applications.
