The Space Communications sector introduces the technologies that enable reliable communication between spacecraft, satellites, ground stations, and mission control centers. Topics include radio frequency systems, telemetry, tracking, command systems, deep-space communications, antenna design, optical communication technologies, and future communication infrastructures for lunar and Martian exploration.
