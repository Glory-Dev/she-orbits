Through expert-led sessions and case studies, participants explore international treaties, commercial space law, resource utilization, satellite governance, sustainability, and emerging policy challenges shaping the future of the global space sector.

## Topics covered

- International Space Law
- Outer Space Treaty
- Commercial Space Regulations
- Space Sustainability
- Lunar & Asteroid Resource Governance
- Ethics in Space Exploration
- Policy Development

## Sessions & milestones

- Expert-led lectures
- Treaty and case study workshops
- Policy development clinics
- Cohort showcase
