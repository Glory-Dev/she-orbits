An intensive workshop introducing participants to the fundamentals of astronomy and modern astrophysics. The workshop explores stellar evolution, galaxies, black holes, cosmology, exoplanets, observational astronomy, and current discoveries that are transforming our understanding of the universe. Participants will engage in interactive sessions led by experts while learning about modern astronomical research techniques.

## Topics covered

- Stellar evolution and galaxies
- Black holes and cosmology
- Exoplanets and planetary systems
- Observational astronomy
- Modern astronomical research techniques

## Sessions & milestones

- Foundations of modern astronomy
- Cosmology and deep space
- Interactive observation and analysis
- Astrophysics research capstone
