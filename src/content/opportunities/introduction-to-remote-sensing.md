Developed in collaboration with Planetek Italia, Introduction to Remote Sensing introduces participants to the fundamentals of Earth observation and satellite remote sensing. Participants explore satellite imaging, geospatial analysis, image interpretation, environmental monitoring, disaster management, and climate-related applications while gaining practical exposure to industry-relevant datasets and methodologies. The course demonstrates how remote sensing and Earth observation technologies support scientific research, environmental protection, sustainable development, and informed decision-making across multiple sectors.
