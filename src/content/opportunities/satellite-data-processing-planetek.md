Developed in collaboration with Planetek Italia, this advanced training program builds upon the fundamentals of Earth observation by introducing participants to professional satellite data processing and geospatial analysis techniques. The program covers satellite image preprocessing, classification, change detection, GIS integration, environmental monitoring, and real-world applications of Earth observation data using industry-standard methodologies and tools.

## Topics covered

- Satellite image preprocessing & calibration
- Classification & change detection algorithms
- GIS integration & spatial data workflows
- Environmental monitoring applications
- Real-world Earth observation data analytics

## Sessions & milestones

- Earth observation fundamentals & data acquisition
- Satellite preprocessing & radiometric calibration
- Classification & geospatial analysis labs
- Applied environmental monitoring capstone
