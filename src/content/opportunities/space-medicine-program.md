The Space Medicine Program explores the medical and physiological challenges associated with human spaceflight. Participants will study the effects of microgravity, radiation, isolation, and long-duration missions on human health while learning about astronaut healthcare, biomedical monitoring, countermeasures, and the future of medicine for lunar and Mars exploration.

## Topics covered

- Microgravity physiology
- Radiation and astronaut health
- Biomedical monitoring
- Countermeasures
- Lunar and Mars medicine

## Sessions & milestones

- Foundations of space medicine
- Case studies from human spaceflight
- Countermeasure design workshop
- Capstone briefing
