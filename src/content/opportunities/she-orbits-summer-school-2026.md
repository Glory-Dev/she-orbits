Designed by leading experts, the Summer School combines technical lectures, interactive workshops, research discussions, and collaborative projects. Curriculum spans orbits, black holes, autonomous spacecraft and AI, space robotics, astronomy and astrophysics, astrobiology and exoplanets, space law and policy, quantum mechanics in space, and multi-body dynamics and control.

## Topics covered

- 9 weeks of specialized space education
- International speakers and industry experts
- Hands-on projects and research activities
- Certificate of Completion
- Global networking opportunities

## Sessions & milestones

- Week 1 – Orbits
- Week 2 – Black Holes
- Week 3 – Autonomous Spacecraft, Formation Flying & AI
- Week 4 – Space Robotics & On-Orbit Servicing
- Week 5 – Astronomy & Astrophysics
- Week 6 – Astrobiology & Exoplanets
- Week 7 – Space Law & Policy
- Week 8 – Quantum Mechanics in Space
- Week 9 – Multi-Body Dynamics & Control
