**SHE ORBITS | October 4–10, 2026**

## A Seven-Day Journey Into the Future of Space Exploration

What does it take to turn an idea into a rocket, a mission, or a future in space?

From October 4–10, 2026, SHE ORBITS celebrated World Space Week under the theme **“Rocket Revolution,”** bringing the excitement of space exploration closer to our community through a seven-day journey of discovery, interactive challenges, career exploration, and expert-led discussions.

Throughout the week, we explored the science, technologies, people, and ideas behind rocket innovation and space missions. Our goal was to help participants look beyond the launch itself and discover the many disciplines, career pathways, and opportunities that make space exploration possible.

## The Seven Days of Rocket Revolution

### Day 1 — The Revolution: What Would You Launch?

We began by inviting our community to imagine the possibilities of space exploration. Participants were encouraged to think beyond existing missions, share their ideas, and consider what they would launch if they had the opportunity to shape the future of space.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-1" alt="Day 1 — The Revolution: What Would You Launch?"}

### Day 2 — Beyond the Flame: Understanding Propulsion

We explored the ideas behind rocket propulsion through an accessible, simplified learning experience. By breaking down a complex engineering concept into an easy-to-understand explanation, we encouraged participants to discover how rockets generate the motion needed to travel beyond Earth.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-2" alt="Day 2 — Beyond the Flame: Understanding Propulsion"}

### Day 3 — Build the Rocket: From Ideas to Engineering

The journey moved from understanding to designing. Through a rocket-design challenge, participants were encouraged to think like engineers, consider the components of a rocket, and explore how creativity and technical thinking come together to transform concepts into possible designs.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-3" alt="Day 3 — Build the Rocket: From Ideas to Engineering"}

### Day 4 — Mission Control: Making Critical Decisions

Space missions require continuous monitoring, precise decisions, and effective problem-solving. Through a mission-control scenario involving a spacecraft attitude deviation, participants were invited to consider how mission teams identify unexpected behavior, assess possible causes, and respond to protect mission objectives.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-4" alt="Day 4 — Mission Control: Making Critical Decisions"}

### Day 5 — Opening the Gates: Expanding Access to Space

We turned the conversation toward accessibility and opportunity. Participants were invited to imagine what they would build if barriers to entering the space sector were reduced, exploring how wider access to knowledge, resources, and collaboration could unlock new ideas and possibilities.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-5" alt="Day 5 — Opening the Gates: Expanding Access to Space"}

### Day 6 — The People Behind the Rocket: Discovering Space Careers

A rocket is never the achievement of one discipline alone. We highlighted the diverse professionals who make space missions possible and introduced participants to different career pathways across engineering, software, research, robotics, business, and policy.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-6" alt="Day 6 — The People Behind the Rocket: Discovering Space Careers"}

### Day 7 — The Next Launch: Imagining 2045

We concluded the journey by looking toward the future. Participants were challenged to imagine space exploration in 2045, consider the missions and technologies that might define the next generation, and share their vision of what humanity could achieve next.

::slideshow{folder="/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/day-7" alt="Day 7 — The Next Launch: Imagining 2045"}

## Expert Sessions: Exploring Human Life Beyond Earth

Rocket Revolution also brought the conversation beyond rockets and spacecraft to the people who may one day live, work, and conduct research beyond our planet. Through two expert-led sessions, we explored the human-health and biological dimensions of space exploration.

![Expert Sessions](/assets/images/world-space-week-2026/sessions.webp)

### Telehealth in Orbit — Carmen Daniella

*Space Medicine | 16:00–17:00 GMT*

This session explored the importance of space medicine and telehealth in supporting human health during space missions. As astronauts operate farther from Earth and access to conventional medical facilities becomes more limited, remote healthcare, medical monitoring, and innovative approaches to delivering care become increasingly important.

### Biological Impact — Mara Selena

*Space Biology & Microbiology | 14:00–15:00 GMT*

This session focused on the biological dimensions of space exploration, highlighting the importance of understanding how space environments affect living organisms and why biological research is essential for preparing for longer and more ambitious missions.

Together, the sessions emphasized that advancing space exploration requires more than developing vehicles and propulsion systems. It also requires understanding and protecting the biological systems that make human exploration possible.

## The Mission Behind Rocket Revolution

At SHE ORBITS, we believe that the future of space exploration depends not only on technological progress, but also on accessible knowledge, diverse perspectives, and opportunities for people to discover where they belong in the space sector.

Rocket Revolution brought these ideas together through a week of learning, imagination, career discovery, and scientific discussion. From the fundamentals of propulsion to the complexities of mission control, from emerging career pathways to the challenges of human health beyond Earth, every day offered another perspective on the work behind space exploration.

**Seven days. Seven career roadmaps. Two expert sessions. One shared vision: making the future of space more accessible, understandable, and inspiring.**

The week may have ended, but the journey continues. At SHE ORBITS, we will keep creating opportunities for our community to learn, explore, connect, and contribute to the next chapter of space exploration.

**SHE ORBITS — The future of space has room for you.**
