The ARTEMIS II Program is a specialized educational and industrial program inspired by NASA's Artemis missions and the future of human lunar exploration. Participants will explore the technologies, mission architecture, lunar operations, life support systems, navigation, habitat design, and scientific objectives that will shape humanity's return to the Moon and pave the way for future Mars missions.

## Topics covered

- NASA Artemis missions & lunar exploration
- Mission architecture & lunar operations
- Life support systems & navigation
- Habitat design & lunar base concepts
- Scientific objectives & Mars pathway

## Sessions & milestones

- Artemis mission architecture & flight profile
- Lunar surface operations & life support
- Navigation, guidance & orbital mechanics
- Habitat design & lunar science capstone
