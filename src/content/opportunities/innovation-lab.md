Innovation Lab is an interdisciplinary research and innovation program designed to transform ideas into impactful solutions for space and related industries. Participants identify real-world challenges, conduct research, develop concepts, and work collaboratively toward practical solutions using engineering principles, emerging technologies, and systems thinking. The program emphasizes innovation, design thinking, teamwork, entrepreneurship, and project development, empowering participants to move from ideas to tangible solutions and contribute to the evolving global space economy.
