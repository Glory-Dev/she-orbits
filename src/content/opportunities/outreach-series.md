The Outreach Series is SHE ORBITS’ global science communication initiative connecting students, educators, professionals, and the wider public with experts from the space sector. Through webinars, seminars, public lectures, awareness campaigns, and educational events, the series explores topics including space science, aerospace engineering, astronomy, Earth observation, and emerging technologies. The initiative seeks to increase scientific literacy, encourage curiosity, and make high-quality space education more accessible to learners from diverse backgrounds around the world.
