AI & Data Science in Space Exploration introduces participants to the growing role of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics across the space sector. The course combines foundational concepts with practical aerospace applications, including satellite imagery, remote sensing, predictive analytics, autonomous spacecraft, and intelligent mission planning. Participants develop an understanding of how data-driven technologies can address complex aerospace challenges and support scientific research, satellite operations, and the future of commercial space missions.

## Topics covered

- Machine learning for space
- Satellite imagery analysis
- Predictive analytics
- Autonomous systems
- Mission planning

## Sessions & milestones

- AI foundations
- Remote sensing datasets
- Autonomy case studies
- Applied project demo
