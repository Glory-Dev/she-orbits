The SHE ORBITS Ambassadors Program is a global leadership initiative designed to empower passionate students and young professionals to represent SHE ORBITS within their universities, organizations, and local communities. Ambassadors will lead outreach activities, organize educational events, promote space awareness, build local partnerships, and inspire the next generation of space professionals while developing leadership, communication, and project management skills.

## Topics covered

- Leadership and outreach
- Community partnerships
- Event organization
- Space awareness campaigns
- Project management

## Sessions & milestones

- Ambassador onboarding
- Outreach toolkit training
- Local activation planning
- Global ambassador summit
