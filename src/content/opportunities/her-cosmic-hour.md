Her Cosmic Hour is SHE ORBITS’ flagship inspirational and educational program dedicated to highlighting the achievements, experiences, and leadership journeys of women across the global space sector. Through keynote talks, fireside conversations, panel discussions, and interactive sessions, participants connect with astronauts, engineers, scientists, entrepreneurs, researchers, and policymakers. The program aims to inspire women to pursue opportunities in STEM and aerospace while creating meaningful connections, expanding professional networks, and showcasing diverse pathways into the space industry.
