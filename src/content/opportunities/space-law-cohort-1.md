The Space Law Program provides participants with a comprehensive introduction to the legal, regulatory, ethical, and policy frameworks governing activities beyond Earth. Through expert-led sessions and case studies, participants explore international space treaties, commercial space regulations, satellite governance, liability, space sustainability, resource utilization, and emerging legal challenges. The program also examines the evolving legal landscape surrounding lunar exploration, private spaceflight, and future human settlements, providing participants with a foundation for understanding the policies shaping the rapidly expanding global space industry.
