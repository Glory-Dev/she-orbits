SHE ORBITS will organize participation in the NASA Space Apps Challenge, the world's largest global hackathon. Participants will collaborate in multidisciplinary teams to develop innovative solutions addressing real-world challenges using NASA's open data, scientific resources, and emerging technologies. The event encourages creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and innovation while connecting participants with a global community of scientists, engineers, designers, and innovators.

## Topics covered

- NASA open data and scientific resources
- Multidisciplinary team collaboration
- Creative problem-solving
- Emerging technologies and prototyping
- Global networking with space professionals

## Sessions & milestones

- Hackathon kickoff & team formation
- NASA data & mentor office hours
- Solution prototyping sprint
- Project showcase & judging
