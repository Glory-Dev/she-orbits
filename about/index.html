About SHE ORBITS

SHE ORBITS.

SHE ORBITS is a multidisciplinary Space & STEM global company to empower females in all space sectors to make Space Industry accessible, inclusive, and inspiring for learners across backgrounds. We aim to build a global community of future space scientists, engineers, researchers, and thinkers by combining foundational theory, real-world context, and interdisciplinary exploration. SHE ORBITS was established in September 2025.

Founded by E&D TSI Astronaut Candidate and Aerospace Engineer Asmaa Hossam, our organisation connects women with world-class female leaders through workshops, mentorships, programs, competitions, real-world research experiences and so more.

Through more than 30 space and STEM sectors, SHE ORBITS introduces learners to aerospace engineering, space medicine, AI and data science, sustainability, policy, communications, business, arts, and future human space fields. These pathways help every participant see where their strengths can contribute to the global space ecosystem.

We envision a future where women are equally represented at every level of the global space industry—as pioneers, innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers, executives, and explorers. Through pioneering programs and initiatives, strategic partnerships, research, education, and workforce development, SHE ORBITS strives to cultivate the next generation of scientists, engineers, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders.

Our Services

SHE ORBITS delivers world-class educational, research, and innovation services that empower the next generation of space professionals while supporting organizations, institutions, and communities through international collaboration and cutting-edge space initiatives.

  • Space workshops and STEM education
  • Space Professional Training
  • Research & Innovation
  • Mentorship programs with women in space industry
  • Global challenges and inspiration series
  • Research experiences and hands-on learning
  • Future CubeSat, analog mission, astronaut training, and aerospace engineering pathways
  • International Workshops & Conferences
  • Industry & Academic Partnerships
  • Space Consulting & Technical Advisory
  • Earth Observation & Geospatial Solutions
  • Research Mentorship & Career Development
  • Career Path & Career opportunities
  • Innovation & Entrepreneurship
  • Global Community & Leadership Programs
  • Outreach & Science Communication
