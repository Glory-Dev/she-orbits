Our Research

Our Research Projects

SHE ORBITS advances interdisciplinary research across autonomy, satellite systems, orbital dynamics, astronaut health, and Earth observation—turning ambitious ideas into publishable, mission-ready insight.

Cyber-Resilient Satellite Collision Avoidance via Deep Reinforcement Learning: An End-to-End PPO Framework with Adversarial Robustness

An AI-driven research project developing an autonomous satellite collision avoidance framework for Low Earth Orbit (LEO) using Deep Reinforcement Learning and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). The system enables intelligent, fuel-efficient maneuver planning while enhancing resilience against adversarial cyber threats, contributing to safer and more autonomous satellite operations.

Deep Reinforcement Learning Autonomous Satellite Navigation Space Cybersecurity

Design and Operations of Small Satellites in Low Earth Orbit

A Pre-Phase A/Phase A conceptual design study for a generic 12U LEO CubeSat bus, covering orbit selection, subsystem architecture (ADCS, EPS, C&DH, structures/thermal), mass/power budgets, and the full mission lifecycle from fabrication through launch, operations, and end-of-life disposal in compliance with debris mitigation guidelines.

CubeSat design LEO mission lifecycle Small satellite systems engineering

Dynamics and Control of Spacecraft Formation Flying in Retrograde Orbits

A technical review examining relative-motion dynamics (HCW, T-H, Yamanaka-Ankersen) and control strategies (PID, LQR, MPC, distributed/cooperative) for spacecraft formation flying in retrograde orbital regimes, with emphasis on J2 and third-body perturbations, collision avoidance, and emerging machine learning/multi-agent RL approaches for autonomous GNC.

Spacecraft formation flying Retrograde orbit dynamics Autonomous GNC

Effects of Space Radiation on Human Health in Low Earth Orbit

A literature review examining how galactic cosmic rays, solar particle events, and trapped radiation affect astronaut health in LEO (with ISS and Tiangong as case studies), covering molecular, cellular, CNS, cardiovascular, ocular, and reproductive effects, current shielding/countermeasure strategies, and key research gaps such as the lack of long-term human dose-response data.

Space radiation biology Astronaut health risks LEO dosimetry

Exploring Lagrange Points for Space Observation Missions

A study of the five Lagrange points arising from the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem, covering their derivation, stability properties (unstable collinear vs. stable triangular points), and their use in real missions—including SOHO and DSCOVR at L1, JWST, WMAP, and Planck at L2, and Chang'e 4's Queqiao relay at Earth-Moon L2—along with future prospects for L4/L5 as sites for stable long-term structures.

Lagrange points Three-body problem Space telescope orbits

Satellite Coverage Analysis for Disaster Monitoring: A Review of Orbital Mechanics, Ground Track Analysis and Satellite Observation Techniques

This study analyzes satellite coverage, ground tracks, and revisit times for disaster monitoring, using RISAT-1 over flood-prone Assam as the primary case study (with MENA and China's Jianghan Plain as secondary regions). It evaluates SAR-based flood detection and satellite pass patterns to assess how effectively different orbital configurations support real-time disaster response.

Satellite Coverage SAR Flood Detection Revisit Time Analysis

Legal Counsel Memo – Launch of an Italian Private AI-Enabled Earth Observation Satellite

A legal roadmap for an Italian New Space startup developing an AI-enabled Earth observation satellite, covering authorization, registration, liability, cybersecurity, export controls, debris mitigation, and AI governance.

AI in Space Earth Observation Space Regulation

The Artemis Accords and Space Resource Extraction

Examines the legal framework surrounding space resource extraction under the Artemis Accords and the Outer Space Treaty, including resource utilization, safety zones, and competing international approaches.

Artemis Accords Space Resources International Law

The End of the ISS? Legal Implications of the Deorbiting of the International Space Station

Explores the legal and environmental implications of ISS deorbiting, including international responsibility, jurisdiction, liability, safety, and potential marine environmental impacts.

ISS Liability Space Sustainability

Space Tourism and Criminal Liability: Who Is Responsible When a Crime Happens in Space?

Investigates criminal jurisdiction in commercial space tourism and proposes an international framework for determining jurisdiction, cooperation, evidence handling, and responsibility for crimes in space.

Space Tourism Criminal Law Jurisdiction

From Prevention to Action: The Challenge of Space Debris

Examines the transition from preventing space debris to actively remediating it, addressing ownership, liability, jurisdiction, authorization, and the governance of active debris removal.

Space Debris ADR Space Sustainability

Iridium 33 – Kosmos 2251

Analyzes the 2009 collision between Iridium 33 and Kosmos 2251, focusing on fault, negligence, liability, collision avoidance, and the legal challenges created by orbital debris.

Orbital Collision Liability Space Debris

Space Insurance

Explores insurance across the space mission lifecycle, from pre-launch and launch to in-orbit operations and third-party liability, highlighting its role in managing commercial space risks.

Space Insurance Risk Management Liability

Before Liftoff: Legal Counsel’s Roadmap for Launching a Commercial Earth-Observation Satellite

Provides a practical legal roadmap for a commercial Earth-observation mission, covering launch licensing, remote sensing, spectrum, registration, insurance, cybersecurity, export controls, and debris mitigation.

Earth Observation Commercial Space Licensing

Policy Brief for a First Private Satellite Mission

Proposes a regulatory framework for a government authorizing its first private satellite mission, addressing licensing, registration, insurance, ITU requirements, cybersecurity, debris, and national security.

Satellite Licensing Regulation Policy

Legal Memorandum for a Startup: Pre-Launch Compliance

Examines the legal requirements facing an Italian Earth-observation startup before launch, including authorization, licensing, registration, insurance, spectrum, export controls, and end-of-life obligations.

Compliance Licensing Earth Observation

Kosmos 954 – Damage Caused by a Space Object

Analyzes the 1978 Kosmos 954 nuclear satellite accident and the resulting radioactive contamination in Canada under the Outer Space Treaty and Liability Convention.

Kosmos 954 Liability Nuclear Spacecraft

The 2021 Russian ASAT Test: Space Security, Responsibility, and the Challenge of Protecting the Orbital Environment

Examines the destruction of Cosmos 1408, the resulting orbital debris, and the international legal questions surrounding state responsibility, liability, and destructive ASAT testing.

ASAT Orbital Debris State Responsibility

The 2021 Russian ASAT Test and International Legal Governance

Studies the legal implications of the 2021 Russian ASAT test, focusing on orbital debris, state responsibility, attribution, and the need for stronger international restrictions.

ASAT Space Governance Orbital Debris

Personal Reflection on AI and Space Law Making

Explores emerging legal challenges created by autonomous AI systems in space, including collision risks, fault-based liability, safety audits, transparency, and algorithmic decision-making.

AI Space Law Autonomous Systems

SpaceX Falcon 9 Moon Crash

Examines the legal implications of a spent Falcon 9 upper stage impacting the Moon, particularly the distinction between physical alteration of celestial bodies and legally compensable damage.

Lunar Activities Liability Space Resources

Advising a Government That Wants to Authorize Its First Private Satellite Mission

Provides a government-focused framework for authorizing and supervising a private satellite mission under international space law and national regulatory requirements.

Authorization Supervision Space Regulation

2030 Space Program: The Need for a Stronger Legal Framework

Analyzes future space initiatives up to 2030, emphasizing regulatory gaps, international cooperation, and policy adaptations required for sustainable space operations.

Space Policy 2030 Strategy Legal Framework
