Charting the future of women in space through purposeful innovation and global collaboration

Mission & Vision

01
Our Purpose

Our Mission

Empowering women in space industry

At SHE ORBITS, our mission is to bridge the gender gap in the global space ecosystem by empowering women and girls through accessible, high-quality education, hands-on technical training, research opportunities, mentorship, and international collaboration.

We are committed to building an inclusive community where aspiring scientists, engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and future astronauts can develop the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to lead innovation in the space sector. Through interdisciplinary learning, industry partnerships, and real-world experiences, we create pathways that transform ambition into meaningful impact for both Earth and space.

02
Our Future

Our Vision

A Universe of Equal Opportunities

We envision a future where women are equally represented at every level of the global space industry—as pioneers, innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers, executives, and explorers.

A world where barriers to opportunity no longer define who contributes to space exploration and technological advancement. Through pioneering programs and initiatives, strategic partnerships, research, education, and workforce development, SHE ORBITS strives to cultivate the next generation of scientists, engineers, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders.

03
Our Values

Core Values That Drive Us

The principles that guide our company's mission to empower women and girls in space and STEM fields.

Inclusion & Diversity

We embrace diversity and ensure equal opportunities for all girls, regardless of their background, ethnicity, or economic status.

Innovation

We foster creativity and encourage groundbreaking ideas that push the boundaries of what's possible in space exploration.

Empowerment

We empower girls to become confident leaders and innovators in STEM fields through mentorship and hands-on experiences.

Collaboration

We build strong networks and partnerships globally to create opportunities for girls in the space industry.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in education and training, providing world-class programs and resources.

Global Impact

We work to create lasting positive change in communities worldwide through our programs and initiatives.

04
Our Goals

Strategic Roadmap 2025–2030

Driving innovation, research, education, and international collaboration to shape the future of the global space industry.

Expand Global Impact

Continue expanding SHE ORBITS' international presence through educational programs, research collaborations, and strategic partnerships, building a global community of future space leaders.

Advance Research & Innovation

Lead interdisciplinary research in artificial intelligence, autonomous space systems, Earth observation, satellite technologies, sustainability, and future human space exploration.

Develop the SHE ORBITS Academy

Build a comprehensive learning ecosystem offering professional courses, certifications, research training, and specialized programs across every major space discipline.

Launch Space Missions & Engineering Projects

Design and develop CubeSat missions, satellite technologies, mission concepts, robotics projects, and future space engineering initiatives through collaborative research and innovation.

Build Global Partnerships

Strengthen collaborations with universities, research institutions, government agencies, industry leaders, and international organizations to accelerate scientific advancement and educational impact.

Empower the Next Generation

Provide accessible pathways through education, mentorship, leadership programs, ambassador initiatives, innovation challenges, scholarships and career development opportunities for future space professionals worldwide.

Our Vision for 2030

By 2030, SHE ORBITS aims to be recognized as a leading global space company advancing space industry, space missions, research, innovation, and international collaboration, while empowering the next generation of scientists, engineers, entrepreneurs, and leaders shaping humanity's future in space.

Empowering women to lead humanity's journey to the stars. We are a pioneering space company dedicated to breaking barriers and creating opportunities for women in aerospace technology, research, and exploration.
